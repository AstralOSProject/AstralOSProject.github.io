Xiaomi Redmi Note 8

codename: ginkgo

IMPORTANT: Unlocking the bootloader requires Xiaomi's Mi Unlock tool (wait times of up to 30 days) and permanently wipes all data on the device. The phone must be running the required Android 11 stock firmware before installing. An unlocked bootloader is required to install AstralOS.

Special boot modes

  • Recovery: With the device powered off, hold Volume Up + Power. Keep holding both buttons until the “MI” logo appears on the screen, then release.
  • Bootloader/Fastboot: With the device powered off, hold Volume Down + Power. Keep holding both buttons until the word “FASTBOOT” appears on the screen, then release.

Installation

  1. Prepare the phone. It must be on the required Android 11 stock firmware. Enable USB debugging (Settings → About phone → tap MIUI version 7 times, then Developer options), verify calls and SMS work on stock, and remove all Google accounts to avoid Factory Reset Protection.

  2. Unlock the bootloader. Create a Mi account, link the device in Settings → Additional settings → Developer options → Mi Unlock status, then use Xiaomi's Mi Unlock tool on a Windows PC. The tool may impose a wait of up to 30 days. Unlocking wipes the device.

  3. Download the files. Save the AstralOS zip from the Downloads section above, and keep the matching recovery.img from the release assets next to it on your PC.

  4. Boot to fastboot. Power off the phone, then hold Volume Down + Power until “FASTBOOT” appears. Connect it to your PC and check the connection:

    fastboot devices

  5. Flash the AstralOS recovery:

    fastboot flash recovery recovery.img

  6. Boot straight to recovery — do not boot the system, as it would overwrite the freshly flashed recovery. Hold Volume Up + Power until the “MI” logo appears, then release. Perform Format data / factory reset. If touch does not respond in recovery, use the volume keys to move and the power key to select.

  7. Sideload the zip. In recovery choose Apply update → Apply from ADB, then run on your PC (ignore a stopped-at-47% message — it is normal):

    adb -d sideload AstralOS-1.0-OFFICIAL-ginkgo.zip

  8. Reboot. Go back and select Reboot system now. The first boot can take up to 15 minutes.

Updating to a newer build

  • Boot to recovery (step 6 key combo) and sideload the new zip exactly like step 7 — no data wipe is needed for same-version updates.
  • If anything misbehaves after an update, format data is NOT required — wipe cache/dalvik in recovery first, your data stays intact.
