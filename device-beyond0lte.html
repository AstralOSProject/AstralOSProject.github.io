Samsung Galaxy S10e

codename: beyond0lte

IMPORTANT: OEM unlocking permanently trips the Knox counter (Samsung Pay, Secure Folder and warranty are affected) and wipes the entire device. Remove all Google and Samsung accounts before unlocking. AstralOS must be flashed on a device running the latest stock firmware (Android 12, SM-G970F).

Special boot modes

  • Recovery: With the device powered off and the USB cable connected to your PC, hold Volume Up + Bixby + Power.
  • Download (Odin) mode: With the device powered off, hold Volume Down + Bixby + Power while connecting the USB cable to your PC.

Installation

  1. Prepare the phone. It must be on the latest stock firmware (Android 12, SM-G970F). In Developer options enable OEM unlocking, then remove every Google and Samsung account from the device.

  2. Boot to Download mode and unlock the bootloader. Power off, hold Volume Down + Bixby + Power while plugging in USB. At the warning screen press Volume Up once to unlock — this wipes the device and trips Knox.

  3. Flash the AstralOS recovery and vbmeta from your PC (with the phone in Download mode):

    heimdall flash --RECOVERY recovery.img --VBMETA vbmeta.img

    Use the recovery.img and vbmeta.img built alongside the AstralOS zip (Heimdall works on Linux/macOS; Odin is the Windows alternative).

  4. Boot to recovery. With the USB cable connected to your PC, hold Volume Up + Bixby + Power until the recovery appears, then perform a Factory reset.

  5. Sideload the zip. In recovery choose Apply update → ADB sideload, then run on your PC:

    adb -d sideload AstralOS-1.0-OFFICIAL-beyond0lte.zip

  6. Reboot. Select Reboot system now. The first boot takes a few minutes.

Updating to a newer build

  • Sideload the new zip from recovery exactly like step 4–5 above — no data wipe is needed for same-version updates.
  • If anything misbehaves after an update, wipe cache/dalvik in recovery first — your data stays intact.
