LG V30

codename: joan — one build for all V30 variants

IMPORTANT: LG provides no official bootloader unlock — only unofficial methods exist, which are unsupported and used at your own risk. The phone must be running the required Android 9.0 stock firmware, and your model number must match the supported list exactly (H930, H930DS, US998, H931, H932, H933, LS998, VS996, V300L, V300K, V300S).

Special boot modes

  • Recovery: With the device powered off, hold Volume Down + Power until the LG logo appears, then release Power for a second and hold it again until recovery comes up. Accept the factory-reset prompt with the hardware buttons — it does not reset your phone, it only boots the custom recovery.
  • Fastboot: With the device powered off, hold Volume Up + Power.

Known quirks

  • IMS / VoLTE: may need extra setup on some carriers.
  • Device integrity: Play Integrity checks may fail — normal for custom ROMs.

Installation

  1. Check your model. Your model number must be an exact match for the two install methods below — fastboot: H930, H930DS, US998 · root + dd: H931, H932, H933, LS998, VS996, V300L, V300K, V300S.

  2. Prepare the phone. It must be on the required Android 9.0 stock firmware. Enable USB debugging (Settings → About phone → tap Build number 7 times, then Developer options), verify calls and SMS work on stock, and remove all Google accounts to avoid Factory Reset Protection.

  3. Unlock the bootloader (fastboot method only — the root + dd method below does not need it). There is no official LG unlock tool anymore; only unofficial methods exist. They are unsupported — use at your own risk. Unlocking wipes the device.

  4. Download the files. Save the AstralOS zip from the Downloads section above, and keep the matching recovery.img from the release assets next to it on your PC.

  5. Method A — fastboot (H930, H930DS, US998): power off, hold Volume Up + Power to enter fastboot, connect the PC and check:

    fastboot devices

    Then flash the AstralOS recovery:

    fastboot flash recovery recovery.img

    Do not boot the system now — it would overwrite the freshly flashed recovery. Go straight to step 7.

  6. Method B — root + dd (all other models): with stock rooted and USB debugging on, push and write the recovery:

    adb -d push recovery.img /sdcard/recovery.img adb -d shell su dd if=/sdcard/recovery.img of=/dev/block/bootdevice/by-name/recovery reboot recovery

  7. Boot to recovery. Power off, hold Volume Down + Power until the LG logo, release Power for a second, hold it again — accept the factory-reset prompt with the hardware buttons (it does not wipe anything). In recovery, perform Format data / factory reset. If touch does not respond, use volume keys to move and power to select.

  8. Sideload the zip. In recovery choose Apply update → Apply from ADB, then run on your PC (ignore a stopped-at-47% message — it is normal):

    adb -d sideload AstralOS-1.0-OFFICIAL-joan.zip

  9. Reboot. Go back and select Reboot system now. The first boot can take up to 15 minutes.

Updating to a newer build

  • Boot to recovery (step 7 key combo) and sideload the new zip exactly like step 8 — no data wipe is needed for same-version updates.
  • If anything misbehaves after an update, wipe cache/dalvik in recovery first — your data stays intact.
